# Related work整理步骤

## 写作步骤

1. **列出****结构框架**

    1. 例如：xx的定义→xx的好处→xx的分类→xx已经有的研究

2. **列出结构下的****过渡句（论点）**

    1. ⭐️重点，逻辑要完整通顺

    2. 所有的过渡句就是一篇不包含引用的精简related work

3. **填入具体的****论文（论据）****支撑论点**

    1. 格式：引用内容（英文）\+ 中文翻译（可选）\+【论文名字】

    2. 引用内容的不同写法：

        1. 引用文章中的具体某几句：直接摘抄的英文原句\+【论文名字索引】

        2. 引用文章的写作主题：摘抄摘要or intro的原句/用原文中的英文原词概括文章主题然后写成英文\+【论文名字索引】

        3. 非常简单的列举，不用介绍文献内容：直接在过渡句中加【论文名字索引】

    3. 论文名字索引

        1. （推荐）整理好引用文章的bib格式然后直接引用bib索引（bib是什么见“收集文献”小节）

        2. （不太推荐）直接引用文章名字，后面还要再整理bib然后再一一对应

# 完整例子

## Sec2\.1 rhetoric in data visualization

### 修辞的定义

- **修辞是一种沟通交流的艺术。**

    - Rhetoric is the art, practice, and study of human communication\.【lunsford2016everyday】修辞是用于人类交流的艺术、实践和研究。

    - 在十七世纪，柏拉图和亚里士多德等人对修辞学进行了讨论，亚里士多德对rhetoric提出了定义：“Rhetoric may be defined as the faculty of observing in any given case the available means of persuasion\. ”【aristotle1991art】

    - Bogost则指出不同的修辞方式是the practice of using different methods persuasively（通过有说服力的方式运用不同手段的手法）【bogost2010persuasive】



### 修辞的作用

- **传统角度来说，修辞的主要目的是为了说服（persuasive）。**

    - 亚里士多德认为修辞的作用是利用Ethos（人格）、Pathos（情感）和Logos（逻辑）来补足纯逻辑的不足，确保“正确的事情”在复杂的社会现实中能够说服大众。【aristotle1991art】

    - Following the classical model, procedural rhetoric entails persuasion——to change opinion or action\.按照传统模式，程序性修辞旨在进行说服——即改变观点或行为。【bogost2010persuasive】

    

- **Burke进一步提出了“identification”说法用以扩充亚里士多德传统的“说服”（Persuasion），即人们使用修辞手法说服观众认同自身利益并建立联系。**

    - As for the relation between "identification” and "persuasion": we might well keep it in mind that a speaker persuades an audience by the use of stylistic identifications; his act of\.persuasion may be for the purpose of causing the audience to identify itself with the speaker’s interests; and the speaker draws on identification of interests to establish rapport between himself and his audience\. So, there is no chance of our keeping apart the meanings of persuasion, identification \("consubstantiality”\) and communication \(the nature of rhetoric as "addressed"\)\. But, in given instances, one or another of these elements may serve best for extending a line of analysis in some particular direction\. 至于“认同”与“说服”之间的关系：我们不妨记住这样一个事实：演讲者通过运用风格上的认同手段来说服听众；其说服行为可能是为了使听众认同演讲者的利益；而演讲者则利用利益认同来建立自己与听众之间的关系。因此，我们不可能将说服、认同（“同质性”）和交流（作为“针对对象”的修辞的本质）的意义分开。但在特定情况下，其中的一个或另一个要素可能会在某些特定方向上更好地服务于分析的推进。【burke1969rhetoric】

    

- **其他的作用还包括促进认识（recognition/ Epistemic）和进行表达（expression）等。**

    - “In human affairs, then, rhetoric is not a matter of giving effectiveness to truth, but of creating truth\.” （因此，在人类事务中，修辞并非赋予真理以效力，而是创造真理。）【scott1967viewing】

    - 【lakoff2024metaphors】

    - Following the contemporary model, procedural rhetoric entails expression — to convey ideas effectively\. 按照当代模式，程序性修辞旨在表达——即有效地传达思想。【bogost2010persuasive】



### 不同种类的修辞

**修辞可以被分为不同的种类，并且在不同的领域有侧重点。**【bogost2010persuasive】【hullman2011visualization】

- **言语修辞（Verbal Rhetoric）/叙述修辞（narrative rhetoric） **

    - 是通过语言和文字进行说服，常被用于文学领域【nida1990role】【scodel1999verbal】。例如通过transitions of narrative perspective【phelan2005living】等手段

    

- **视觉修辞（Visual Rheoric）**

    - 通过图像、色彩和构图进行说服（如电影、海报），常被用于电影和广告领域等【jeong2008visual】。例如通过visual metaphor【booth1978metaphor】【jeong2008visual】，Semantically related color【caivano2010color】【welhausen2018toward】等手段。



- **游戏领域**

    - **Bogost提出了procedural rhetoric：**程序性修辞是指以有说服力的方式运用过程的修辞手法。“The art of persuasion through rule\-based representations and interactions rather than the spoken word, writing, images, or moving pictures\.”（是通过基于规则的再现和交互，而非言语、文字、图像或动态影像来进行说服的艺术。）【bogost2010persuasive】

    - Procedural rhetoric is a technique for making arguments with computational systems and for unpacking computational arguments others have created\. 程序化修辞是一种利用计算系统进行论证以及剖析他人所创建的计算性论证的技术。【bogost2010persuasive】

    



### **可视化领域中的修辞**

- 对于可视化来说，数据可视化不仅是信息的传达，更是一种复杂的社会交互与说服过程，**而修辞可以推动data democratization。**【kostelnick2008visual】

    - We draw a parallel between narrative visualization interpretation and evidence from framing studies in political messaging, decision\-making, and literary studies\. Devices for understanding the rhetorical nature of narrative information visualizations are presented, informed by the rigorous application of concepts from critical theory, semiotics, journalism, and political theory\. We draw attention to how design tactics represent additions or omissions of information at various levels—the data, visual representation, textual annotations, and interactivity—and how visualizations denote and connote phenomena with reference to unstated viewing conventions and codes\. **Hullman等人系统阐述了理解叙事信息可视化修辞本质的方法体系，提出了提出了叙事可视化的四种修辞类型：**信息获取修辞 \(Information Access Rhetoric\)溯源修辞 \(Provenance Rhetoric\)映射修辞 \(Mapping Rhetoric\)基于语言学的修辞 \(Linguistic\-based Rhetoric\)程序修辞 \(Procedural Rhetoric\)， 着重剖析设计策略如何在数据层、视觉呈现层、文本标注层及交互层面对信息进行增删处理【hullman2011visualization】



### 可视化中已经研究的修辞策略

- **目前，可视化大多从视觉修辞的视角进行研究并建立相关框架和设计策略。**

    - The visual rhetoric of data displays \(e\.g\., charts, graphs, maps\) 【kostelnick2008visual】这篇文章在视觉修辞的框架下探讨了数据可视化设计中不断演变的“清晰度”概念。Indeed the ascendancy of rhetorical approaches was initiated by the perceptual/cognitive science of data design, which in seeking to identify optimal display techniques, fostered a concern for ethics and evoked the universality and minimalism of modernist aesthetics\. The rhetoric of adaptation, which emphasizes the variability of audiences, purposes, and situational contexts, rendered clarity contingent and mutable—a moving target that requires constant attention\. Social rhetoric considered data design as a collective construct, tethering clarity to visual discourse communities, convention\-building, cultural values, and power\. The concept of clarity has been further reoriented by the rhetoric of participation, which is fostered by interactive digital design that enables users to adapt displays according to their needs and interests\. 文中详细对比了四种修辞视角：强调普遍认知规律的科学修辞、注重特定情境需求的适配修辞、建立在文化共识基础上的社会修辞，以及新兴的参与修辞。

    - It illustrates how pathos functions as a rhetorical strategy in contemporary data visualizations, examining the interplay of rhetorical strategies and aesthetic qualities\. 阐释了情感诉求如何在当代数据可视化中作为一种修辞策略发挥作用，同时探讨了修辞策略与美学特质之间的互动关系【prantl2025untangling】

    - Empowered by Aristotle’s linkage between rhetoric and probability to reframe the linkage between Big Data and rhetorical studies, this work examines how, in the age of Big Data, visual rhetoric functions as a mechanism of distillation to find patterns \(or unconceal relationships\) and guide the design of communication\. 通过亚里士多德的古典修辞理论来重新理解大数据可视化，研究了大数据（Big Data）时代背景下，视觉修辞如何作为一种核心机制来构建意义、发现模式并指导传播设计【salvo2012visual】

    - 这篇文章专注于可视化作为一种“说服行为”和“话语构建”的修辞属性。Manchia argues that visualization is never a neutral display of data, but rather relies on two rhetorical strategies—'immediacy' \(making data appear directly presented 'as is'\) or 'transparency' \(making the chart appear as an invisible container or 'crystal goblet'\)—to construct an objective authority\. Through a specific process of visual 'translation' \(putting data into discourse\), it guides the viewer’s cognition and 'pathemic' \(emotional\) effects\.Manchia 指出，可视化并非客观反映现实，而是通过特定的视觉策略来构建权威性和意义。Manchia 认为可视化从来不是中立的数据展示，而是依赖“即时性”（让数据看似直接呈现）或“透明性”（让图表看似隐形容器）这两种修辞策略来构建客观权威，并通过特定的视觉“翻译”引导观者的认知与情感。【manchia2022beyond】

    - lin等人研究了一类特定的可视化——章鱼图的视觉修辞。Employing rhetorical schema theory, we collected and analyzed 90 octopus maps spanning from the 19th century to contemporary times\. We closely examined how octopus maps implement their persuasive intents and constructed a design space that reveals how visual metaphors are strategically constructed and what common rhetorical strategies are applied to components such as maps, octopus imagery, and text\. 收集并分析了 19 世纪至今的 90 幅章鱼图，深入考察其如何实现说服意图，构建出揭示视觉隐喻如何策略性构建的设计空间，并解析地图、章鱼图像及文本等组件中常用的修辞策略。【octopus】



- **也有研究专注某一种具体的修辞手法。**

    - 有研究专门研究隐喻作为一种修辞手法在数据可视化中的作用，包括【Metaphorical Visualization: Mapping data to familiar concepts】【Metaphoric mappings: The art of visualization】【On the role of metaphor in information visualization】并提出一些低层次的设计策略。

    - kim研究思辨可视化，将思辨设计作为一种修辞手法，用于唤起公众对尚未深入探讨的社会议题的关注。Speculative design is an emerging rhetorical strategy in design practices and research to raise public awareness in social agendas that have been little explored\. As a stream of this type of research, we propose speculative visualization that aims to achieve speculative design by utilizing techniques from data visualization and graphic design\. Specifically, speculative visualization represents socially and politically meaningful data in aesthetic ways to provoke viewers’ interpretation and further elicit discussions\.【kim2010speculative】



- **相比之下，我们关注一种之前尚未在可视化中未被充分讨论的修辞视角：程序修辞，探讨其如何能够在可视化中发挥作用。**
