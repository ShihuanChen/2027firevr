Note. The six-stage funnel follows PRISMA guidelines for systematic data collection. Round 1 (S0→S2) uses keyword-based retrieval from three parallel sources (GDELT, Google News RSS, Bing News), URL deduplication, title-based exclusion of false positives (wildfire, gunfire), and a six-factor relevance scoring system. Round 2 (S3→S5) applies a five-dimension rubric (text length, spatial richness, narrative completeness, vulnerable group detail, source authority) with a ≥75 threshold for strong recommendation, followed by VR-narrative segment coding using a 3-axis × 4-dimension framework. The final samples achieve inter-coder reliability of Cohen's κ ≥ 0.80 through dual independent coding and third-coder arbitration.
