TITLE: Abbey View fire :  pure terror  as mum and daughter flee
BODY: Watford Community Housing has been accused of "neglect" after a fire at Meriden flats put one person in hospital and left multiple homes uninhabitable. Eight crews and police swarmed Abbey View on Saturday night (July 26) - three years on from a serious fire at the same 17-storey tower. WCH said staff attended quickly to offer help and reassurance - with six households now in alternative accommodation - adding it will "continue to support all residents". Sharonea Fitzgerald, who lives on the ninth floor with her six-year-old daughter, recalled "pure terror" as they tried to escape. "It was just horrible," the 33-year-old told the Watford Observer. "I opened up my door and was greeted with thick black smoke, it was terrifying. My daughter is scared to be at home so I have had to drive her to my mum's house in Bournemouth and now I am in the flat alone." She accused WCH of "neglect" over how quickly residents were allowed to return, adding: "I feel like they did not really test everything properly before letting everyone back in. "The smell of smoke is so bad and they just sent us back home. They had cleaners do floors 11-17 the next day but did not do the downwards floors, so I have had to clean it myself." Bemoaning the cost of ruined belongings, she said: "This is not the Hamptons, this is the Meriden, and I just feel so let down." A WCH spokesperson said the fire service confirmed the building was safe for residents to return, with the worst-affected areas cleaned beforehand and a full deep-clean of all areas starting this week. It comes just over three years after fire spread through the same building in July 2022 - which eventually saw boys aged 13 and 14 admit arson with recklessness as to whether life was endangered. While the full report into the latest blaze has not yet been published, it is believed it may have been caused by a discarded cigarette.
