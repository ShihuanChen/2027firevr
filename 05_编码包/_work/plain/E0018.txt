TITLE: Major emergency response descends on street after flat fire
BODY: A major emergency response descended on a Southampton street called to a high rise fire. Fire, police and ambulance services attended a high rise building in Centenary Plaza after reports of smoke on the fifth floor at around 2.15pm on Saturday, May 16. John Thornycroft Road in Woolston was closed as multiple crews attended the scene and a fire and rescue service cordon was placed around the building. A spokeswoman for Hampshire Fire and Rescue said: “On arrival it was confirmed that there was a fire within an apartment on the fifth floor. “Firefighters have evacuated the fifth and sixth floor of the building. “The fire is now out and firefighters are using ventilation to clear smoke from the affected areas. “We ask that people avoid the area while emergency services remain in attendance at the incident.” One witness to the scene said: “I’m only here because I'm in the car park, so I can't get back to my car. “I think the fire was out quite quickly.
