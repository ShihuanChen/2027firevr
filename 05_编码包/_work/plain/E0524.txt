TITLE: Massive fire breaks out at Mumbai high - rise ; fire team rushed to spot | Mumba
BODY: MUMBAI: A fire broke out at a high-rise building on SV Road in Mumbai at around 10.50 am on Thursday. The fire brigade declared the incident at JNS Business Center, SV Road, Behrampada, near Gandhi School, Jogeshwari West, as a Level-II fire call. “No injuries have been reported so far,” an official said. Earlier this week, a major fire broke out in a 12-storey residential building in Navi Mumbai’s Vashi shortly after midnight on Monday, killing four people, including an elderly woman and a family of three. The incident took place at Raheja Residency in M G Complex, Sector 14. Ten other residents from the upper floors were hospitalised after suffering from suffocation. Police identified the deceased as Sundar Balakrishnan, 44, his wife Pooja Rajan, 39, and their six-year-old daughter Vedika, who lived on the 12th floor. The fourth victim, 84-year-old Kamala Jain, was bedridden and lived with her son’s family on the 10th floor, where the fire is believed to have started. Authorities said the cause of the blaze is yet to be determined. “Only after the electrical safety department officials submit their report will the further course of action be decided,” said Shashikant Chandekar, senior inspector at Vashi police station.
